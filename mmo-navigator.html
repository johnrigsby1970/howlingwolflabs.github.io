MMO Navigator

Your ultimate map companion for classic and modern MMORPGs.

Available Free on the Microsoft Store

Overview

MMO Navigator takes the guesswork out of exploring massive fantasy worlds. It runs alongside your game to give you live, accurate position tracking on crystal-clear 2D and 3D maps. Easily plot your routes, navigate confusing terrain, and keep track of your exact location on a second monitor or overlay—so you can focus on the adventure, not getting lost.

  • Live Movement Tracking: Follows your character's exact position on the map in real-time as you walk, run, or ride.
  • 2D & 3D Map Views: Switch between clean top-down maps and full 3D terrain views to see elevation and landmarks clearly.
  • Draw Mode: Download player-made maps from fan sites or draw custom 2D zone maps automatically as you explore.
  • Corpse & Dungeon Navigation: Effortlessly find dungeons by coordinate, navigate back to your corpse location, or share points of interest with friends.
